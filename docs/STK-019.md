# STK-019 — Point-in-time instrument identity

Stable instrument identities and namespaced effective-dated mappings support NSE symbol/series, BSE codes and broker IDs without joining unrelated securities. Resolution requires mapping availability by cutoff, half-open validity intervals and a unique identity; future lookups and ambiguity fail closed. Synthetic exchange/ISIN rules prevent fixture identities being treated as real instruments.

Validation: two focused cases passed, covering symbol reuse, namespace separation, late mapping availability, future lookup, conflicting identity and synthetic identity isolation. PostgreSQL persistence is part of the subsequent ingestion ticket. Real ISIN verification/provider imports remain disabled pending source permission and access.

Status: Ready for Release for synthetic functionality; real source access blocked.
