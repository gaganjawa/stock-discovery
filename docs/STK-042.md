## Frozen audit identity — 14 October 2026

Split and label policy identifiers are explicitly frozen independently of the deployed strategy constant. Unknown dataset split policies produce unavailable reports. Duplicate recommendation IDs are rejected; retained IDs and exclusions use deterministic ordering. A content-based audit ID binds dataset/calendar/policy identities, boundaries, assignments and exclusions, and is displayed and exported. It is not an immutable trial ledger or proof of an untouched test period.
