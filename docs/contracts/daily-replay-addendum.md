# Daily replay outcome addendum 1.1.0

Original recommendation contract and historical fixture outcomes remain unchanged. New daily replay envelopes identify schema_version 1.1.0, evaluator/data versions and evaluation time. A fill records interval_start, interval_end and timing (`open` or `within_session_unknown`), rather than inventing an exact intraday timestamp. Forecast and trade envelopes are stored separately from immutable recommendations; the API's reveal view combines these for display.

Synthetic cost schedule SYN-daily-costs-v1 is effective [2020-01-01,2030-01-01): INR 1 brokerage plus INR .10/share per side, adverse .05 entry/stop/time-exit slippage. Targets are conservative limit fills at the target. These are fictional arithmetic assumptions, not actual brokerage or statutory rates. Entry above the frozen 1% cap or target cancels; zero/missing volume invalidates; quantity exceeding 1% of the day's volume invalidates under a disclosed coarse daily-liquidity assumption. Missing sessions/cost schedules and action execution fail closed.

Opening gaps establish open order. Otherwise entry/stop or stop/target order is ambiguous whenever OHLC permits incompatible outcomes. No scalar net profit is assigned; feasible known alternatives are retained. Expiry is exclusive at the third next session's open, allowing two full entry sessions without pretending a close-time touch happened earlier. Three holding sessions include entry day; target/stop takes precedence over time exit. Forecast endpoint is independent of trade exit.

Single-replay equity curves mark open positions and deduct costs. Separate runs start with separate fictional INR 100,000 and cannot be summed into one portfolio. A separate predeclared single-position synthetic portfolio evaluator can combine one cohort with cash/risk revalidation, explicit overlap exclusions and conservative unresolved-path handling. It reports closing-mark exposure rather than calendar-weighted exposure; multi-position/account portfolio integration remains unavailable. Benchmark comparison is undefined until comparable permitted observations exist; cash reference is zero. Synthetic outcomes do not support promotion or profitability claims.
