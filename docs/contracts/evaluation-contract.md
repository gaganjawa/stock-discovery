# Evaluation contract 1.0.0

STK-002 · P1 · v0.1 · proposed evaluation policy `STK-EVAL-1`. This defines measurement, not a replay engine or approval to trade. It consumes immutable recommendations and separately versioned outcomes from [STK-001](recommendation-contract.md). The nine original fixtures are unchanged. Synthetic results validate arithmetic and classification only; they establish neither predictive accuracy nor profitability.

## Evaluation snapshot and units

Freeze an evaluation manifest before a real experiment: recommendation cohort/IDs, instrument universe and historical membership, strategy/config/code/dataset versions, forecast definitions, outcome revision IDs, as-of time, calendar, price/adjustment basis, costs, benchmark, split boundaries and promotion-policy version/hash. Count each initial decision once; linked revisions are separate decision vintages and must not replace poor earlier results or double-count the same position. Include rejected/abstaining recommendations. Group results by strategy, horizon, action and universe; the pooled fixture result below is only arithmetic practice.

Use Decimal money arithmetic; round only display, with half-even rounding to four decimal places for percentages. Rates are fractions in [0,1], displayed as percentages; expectancy is INR/trade. Every metric returns numerator (or sum), denominator, value and reason. A zero denominator returns `value: null, reason: no_eligible_observations`, never zero or a perfect score. Retain exclusion counts/IDs and reasons. Empty input means zero coverage counts and undefined rates, not a successful test.

## Forecast results

Direction reference is the frozen `forecast.reference_price` at `forecast.reference_at`, never an eventual entry fill. For all seven directional STK-001 examples it is raw INR 100.00 at 6 August 2025 10:00Z. Compare with the exact endpoint close at `forecast.end_at`: 11 August 2025 10:00Z. The information cutoff is 6 August 10:05Z. An endpoint must be specified as an actual session/time before the run; do not silently substitute a later quote if missing. Missing endpoint is invalid_data (or pending while awaiting ingestion), and an unelapsed horizon is pending. Corporate actions must use a frozen consistent reference/endpoint basis; absent required adjustments invalidate the comparison.

Realized direction is `up` for endpoint > reference, `down` for endpoint < reference, and `flat` for exact equality at the stored precision. Flat predictions are correct only for equality; an up/down prediction against unchanged prices is incorrect. No inferred flat band or probability. Directional correctness is independent of trading, fees, stop-outs and whether entry occurred. Price change `(endpoint/reference - 1)` is a forecast observation, not an investment return.

Target attainment asks only whether a frozen price level was reached during `(information_cutoff_at, forecast.end_at]`. Freeze a target price, up/down orientation and raw/adjusted basis before evaluation. STK-001 has no separate forecast-target field: for this explicitly labelled **execution-target attainment diagnostic**, reuse the existing six BUY execution targets of 110.00; do not claim these were separately generated forecast probabilities or modify original recommendations. SELL has no target and is inapplicable. A future directional/valuation forecast must explicitly declare its own target; do not invent one from the outcome.

For an up target, any eligible price/high >= target proves attainment; for a down target, price/low <= target does. Endpoint equality counts as attained. A bar must lie wholly within the horizon; a straddling bar without finer data cannot prove an in-window touch. Whether stop occurred first is irrelevant here. The primary rate waits until horizon maturity for both hits and misses to avoid early-success bias. A confirmed in-window touch can prove success despite gaps elsewhere; proving a miss requires complete eligible observations through the horizon. Without a hit and complete coverage, return invalid_data, not false. STK-001 explicitly assumes complete fictional paths; this assumption cannot be transferred to market data. Never infer intrabar ordering from OHLC; a both-barriers bar can prove target attainment but cannot prove target-before-stop.

## Metric definitions and denominators

Each eligibility decision is made independently; missing trade data does not invalidate a known forecast close, and a known entry can remain usable when its later exit is unknown.

| Metric | Numerator | Denominator | Exclusions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direction correctness | Correct direction labels | Matured directional forecasts with valid reference and exact endpoint | No forecast, pending, invalid/missing endpoint or inconsistent basis |
| Target attainment | Proven target touches within frozen horizon | Matured target-bearing forecasts with known hit/miss | No target, unelapsed horizon; unproven misses with missing observations |
| BUY entry-trigger rate | BUY records with a verified eligible opening fill | BUY records whose entry is known: filled, or definitively expired/cancelled without fill | SELL, WAIT, HOLD, insufficient evidence; pending/unknown entry or invalid fill evidence |
| Target-before-stop rate | Target exit first | Executed BUY round trips with resolved, valid first-exit reason (target, stop, gap_stop, maximum_holding_expired) | No entry, open/pending, ambiguous order, invalid/missing exit evidence; holding-sale transactions |
| Net win rate | Eligible round trips with net P&L > 0 | Executed, closed BUY round trips with valid fills and complete costs | Same unresolved/invalid/no-entry cases; incomplete costs/basis; SELL-only proceeds |
| Net expectancy per executed trade | Sum of eligible net P&L in INR | Same completed executed BUY round trips as net win rate | Same exclusions as net win rate; this is **closed-trade expectancy**, not an estimate for unresolved executions |
| Direction coverage | Valid matured direction labels | All directional forecasts whose horizon is due at snapshot | Not-yet-due forecasts and no-forecast records; show these counts separately |
| Target coverage | Known matured target labels | All target-bearing forecasts whose horizon is due | Not-yet-due and no-target records |
| Entry coverage | Known BUY entry results | All BUY records whose entry window is due | Not-yet-due BUY records; report early verified fills separately |
| Trade resolution coverage | Eligible closed BUY P&L observations | Verified BUY executions whose maximum possible holding deadline is due | Not-yet-due executions; report open, ambiguous and invalid due executions explicitly |

Coverage excludes nothing due merely because data is missing. An overdue pending outcome stays in its coverage denominator; unelapsed pending is not due. A fill can be known before entry expiry; the trigger rate may include it but must be labelled provisional for a still-developing cohort. Promotion only uses fully elapsed cohorts with their unresolved counts intact. Due holding deadline is the latest permitted entry session plus `max_holding_sessions - 1`, using the frozen calendar, so a short realized trade cannot selectively mature a cohort early. The walkthrough is evaluated after all entry, forecast and maximum holding deadlines (12 August 2025 close for a latest 8 August entry held three sessions).

Entry expiry without fill is a known non-trigger (included in entry denominator, absent from profitability denominators). Post-entry maximum holding expiry is a real time exit (included in target-before-stop denominator as non-target and profitability according to net P&L). Breakeven net P&L is included in the net-win denominator, not its numerator; report zero-net count separately. Open trades are neither wins nor losses. A `cancelled` outcome needs verified pre-entry cancellation/no fill to count as non-trigger; an uncertain order is pending, not cancelled. An ambiguous case with a verified entry contributes to entry-trigger rate but not to resolved trade rates. Missing trade records are `missing`; no manufactured no-entry. An invalid-data outcome does not establish a fill solely because a fill-shaped object remains in the record; the validity must be separately established.

SELL means closing/reducing an existing long holding, not opening a short. Report sell execution counts/proceeds separately. A complete original acquisition/cost ledger would allow holding-lot return metrics in a separate cohort; the fixture's 99.90 proceeds are not profit and are excluded from BUY round-trip metrics. WAIT, HOLD and insufficient evidence always remain in action/abstention coverage, not silently removed from the universe.

## Cash returns and drawdown

For a resolved long round trip, `gross = quantity × (exit_fill - entry_fill)`, `net = gross - entry_fees - exit_fees`. Executed fill prices already include any adverse slippage; do not deduct it again. If using reference prices instead, apply adverse slippage once to obtain fills and record that transformation. Per-trade net return is `net / (quantity × entry_fill + entry_fees)`. Entry/exit fees must be separately known to compute this denominator; a combined charge alone is insufficient unless its allocation is explicitly frozen. Do not subtract the combined charge from already net P&L. Gaps use actual assumed fills, not nominal stop/trigger levels.

The original `SYN-fill-v1` specifies one share, INR .10 each side, zero spread/slippage. Four resolved BUY trades: -4.20/102.10, 7.80/102.10, .80/102.10 and -9.20/104.10. Their net P&L sum is -4.80 and closed-trade expectancy -1.20 INR. Mean per-trade percentage is not a portfolio return: cash constraints, overlapping exposure, quantity, idle cash and reinvestment differ. Never sum these independent hypothetical scenarios into a portfolio equity curve.

A portfolio curve is a separate chronological ledger: cash plus open positions valued at contemporaneously available marks, plus receivables minus liabilities and accrued costs. Trading cash flows are internal; deposits/withdrawals are external. Use at least each session close and each external-flow boundary, with pre-flow valuation; record the sampling grid because close-only drawdown may miss intraday losses. Specify whether marks include estimated liquidation fees; this fixture uses zero costs and mark-to-market without liquidation fees. If any material position mark is missing/stale beyond the frozen tolerance, curve return/drawdown is undefined with a reason; do not forward-fill silently or drop that point.

For positive starting capital, build a flow-neutral wealth index W starting at 1. At each point let E be total **post-flow** equity, F the signed external flow booked after that point's market movement (deposit positive, withdrawal negative), and Eprev previous post-flow equity. Growth factor is `(E - F) / Eprev`; set `W = Wprev × growth`. Multiple intraperiod flows require their own pre/post valuation boundaries; this formula is not a shortcut for unknown flow timing. Time-weighted portfolio return is `Wlast - 1`. Peak is running max(W), drawdown at each point is `1 - W/peak`, maximum drawdown is the largest such value. Never reset the peak on a deposit. Zero wealth is a 100% loss; negative equity, nonpositive initial/prior equity, withdrawal leaving no capital, or restarting after total loss requires a separate segment and yields undefined for this simple continuous-curve calculation. A one-point curve has insufficient intervals and returns null with reason.

Separate synthetic curve in `examples/evaluation-edge-cases.synthetic.json`: cash 500 plus five shares marked 100 gives E0=1000. Next mark 140 makes pre-flow equity 1200; deposit 500 gives post-flow cash 1000 and E1=1700. Next mark 72 gives pre-flow 1360; withdrawal 200 gives cash 800 and E2=1160. Next mark 95.20 gives E3=1276. Flows are booked at those marks. W = 1, 1.2, .96, 1.056; return **5.6%**, maximum drawdown **20%**. Ending equity minus starting equity is 276 but net contributions are 300, so cash profit is **-24**. Neither the 27.6% account-value change nor -2.4% simple profit/initial capital is the time-weighted return. The open position is never sold; ignoring it would miss the drawdown.

## Benchmarks and comparability

Freeze a **rules-only baseline**: same discovery universe, data cutoffs, quantitative strategy/config, risk/sizing, available cash, executions, costs and calendars, with LLM/news adjustment disabled. Archive both baseline and candidate decisions before outcomes. Compare on paired decision dates and report candidate abstentions; do not compare candidate's selected winners with all baseline trades. A rules-only candidate must additionally face a predeclared simpler rule or no-trade baseline; comparing a policy to itself cannot demonstrate incremental value.

Proposed broad Indian-equity swing market reference: **NIFTY 500 total-return index** for a broad-universe experiment, or a predeclared universe-matched total-return index if scope differs. This is a benchmark specification, not a data fetch or licensing decision. Use identical evaluation dates, currency, dividend treatment, initial capital and external-flow timing. Report full-capital buy-and-hold reference and exposure-matched reference separately: the latter uses the candidate's prior-known exposure schedule and the same stated idle-cash yield, without hindsight weighting. Index levels are a non-investable reference; an investable proxy needs its own fees, slippage and tracking-error assumptions. Do not compare a gross index to a net strategy and call the difference tradable alpha. Record a cash/no-trade reference (fixture yield zero). If comparable permitted benchmark data/costs are unavailable, comparisons are null with reason and promotion evidence is insufficient.

## Frozen evaluation and proposed promotion policy

**Deterministic gates** must all pass: eligible evidence/closed bars; immutable decision and outcome revisions; no future access in analysis; correct units/timezones/corporate actions; independently checked fills and costs; ambiguity/missing-data handling; reproducible dataset/code/config and policy hash; correct capital/exposure ledger; benchmark comparability. A failed deterministic gate yields **reject** for this candidate evaluation, irrespective of returns. Fixes require a new version and rerun; they do not justify silently repairing a held-out result. STK-002 validates formulas only, not the future replay system's gates.

**Proposed engineering policy EP-1**, to be ratified before any real strategy experiment (numbers are operational screening choices, not statistically proven guarantees):

1. Chronologically order eligible decision timestamps. Reserve earliest 60% of complete calendar months for development, next 20% validation, final 20% untouched test; use floor for the first two counts, remainder for test, at least 30 complete months in total. Write the resulting exact UTC boundaries to the manifest before any performance run. Insufficient dated coverage means insufficient_evidence; do not invent dates for these fixtures. Tune only on development; validation selects one candidate once. Freeze it before opening test; if test influences changes, it becomes development evidence and a new untouched period is required.
2. Define each decision's label interval from cutoff through the latest forecast endpoint or latest possible trade exit. Purge every earlier-split decision whose interval reaches the next split. Add a boundary gap of at least the maximum configured label span (including entry validity) between retained decision windows; for calendar-dependent horizons calculate the conservative span from the pinned calendar. Fit preprocessing/normalization only on retained training data. Group simultaneous issuer/strategy decisions, prevent same outcome-window leakage across folds, and keep a dated ledger of every trial. A random split is not acceptable.
3. Require at least 100 resolved BUY round trips in validation and independently 100 in untouched test, spanning at least 6 calendar months in each after purging (longer raw history may therefore be necessary); require at least 100 matured directional forecasts and 100 matured target-bearing forecasts in each when those claims are made. Require at least 20 distinct decision sessions and 20 instruments per historical evaluation cohort; if the strategy's declared scope cannot satisfy this, adopt a different policy **before** results, not a post hoc exemption. Counts are not effective independent sample sizes; overlapping positions and correlated equities reduce information.
4. Require direction, target and entry coverage each >=95% where applicable, and due-trade resolution >=95%; ambiguous plus invalid/missing due executed-trade outcomes must be <=5% of due executions, with each category disclosed (open/pending unresolved also fail resolution coverage). Coverage must be reported for all prespecified instruments/periods, not just selected subsets. Missing benchmarks or an undefined required metric means insufficient_evidence.
5. For both validation and untouched test: closed-trade net expectancy >0; cash-constrained time-weighted net portfolio return >0 and >= both the rules-only and exposure-matched market reference; maximum flow-neutral drawdown <=10%; net expectancy remains >0 under twice the predeclared fees and adverse slippage. Record absolute and excess results and full-capital market reference. No minimum directional accuracy, target rate or net win rate is required: these diagnostics alone do not establish edge. Cost stress is a separate rerun, not a second deduction in base P&L. Risk limits/allocation still require STK-003.
6. Freeze the selected candidate for forward paper operation before observing its results. Require at least 3 calendar months, 50 resolved executions and, when applicable, 50 matured forecasts/targets across at least 20 sessions and 10 instruments; the same coverage and performance conditions apply. Historical LLM contamination is another reason forward evidence is mandatory even though no model is called in this ticket. Paper fills/cost realism must pass deterministic checks; paper approval never enables orders.

Decision order: deterministic failure → **reject**; otherwise absent/immature sample, low coverage, undefined metrics, unresolved benchmark/rights, unratified policy or missing forward evidence → **insufficient_evidence**; otherwise any prespecified performance condition fails → **reject**; otherwise → **promote** to the next explicitly named research/paper lifecycle stage after owner review, never automatic capital allocation or live trading. A known deterministic failure is not hidden behind a small sample. Record all reasons; never relax a threshold because a candidate nearly passed. Large samples with strongly dependent outcomes may still be insufficient; inspect results by month, sector and regime and report paired block-based uncertainty before owner review. EP-1's minima do not guarantee statistical significance or economic durability.

These numerical gates were specified without searching or optimizing the fictional outcomes. The fixtures are deliberately mixed arithmetic cases, not development, validation, test or paper samples, and cannot satisfy promotion evidence. Outstanding product policy decisions are EP-1 ratification, actual universe/benchmark data route and exact date boundaries once a permitted dataset exists, plus capital/risk settings in STK-003. No such dependency blocks delivery of this contract.

## Executable verification

Run `python3 scripts/validate_evaluation_examples.py` and the unchanged `python3 scripts/validate_contract_examples.py`. The new script uses only the standard library and existing parsing/Decimal helpers. It validates the original fixtures before reading them, derives individual eligibility and aggregate arithmetic, and compares with independently written constants. The small separate evaluation fixture covers costs turning a gross gain into net loss, net breakeven, unresolved/missing outcomes, unchanged prices, no eligible observations and the open-position drawdown/flow example. It does not generate fills, infer ordering, call a model or fetch prices. Target diagnostics use only the original explicitly complete fictional paths. Portfolio calculations refuse missing marks. This demonstrates contract correctness, not an implemented general-purpose backtester or measured prediction accuracy.
